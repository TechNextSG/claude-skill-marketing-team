# marketing-team

Run TechNext's AI marketing team - Pixel (designer: 3 drips + a 3:4 social post a day), Razel (lead researcher: 20 qualified PH + SG leads a day to the Leads tab) and Ralph (social media planner: 1 post a day for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn from approved posts). Works the HQ on this PC (http://localhost:3985, hq.py), keeps the AI Marketing HQ sheet and the Marketing Hub AI team page up to date, and keeps improving the agents' skills. Use when the user says "run the team", "run Pixel/Razel/Ralph", "/marketing-team", "make a drip", "find leads", "plan a post", "check the HQ", or gives the team a job.
